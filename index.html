A Comparative Reproducibility Study of Jev and Leading Reranking Models

Every method reranks the same BM25 top-100 on 14 datasets from TREC DL, BEIR, and BRIGHT. Jev, a non-generative decision model, is run in three modes — Noul, Score, and Choice — and compared with neural rerankers and with GPT-4o-mini given the same instructions. Under review.
